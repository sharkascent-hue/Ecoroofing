About Eco Roofing

A family-run roofing company built on trust.

Eco Roofing is a family-run business in Dublin with over 15 years’ experience, known for reliable work, quality craftsmanship and honest, transparent pricing.

We do everything from a single slipped tile to a complete new roof, for homes and businesses across Dublin, Wicklow, Kildare, Meath and Louth.

FAQ

Roofing questions, answered.

The questions homeowners ask us most. Anything else, call us on 087 130 6744.

How much does a roof repair cost in Dublin?

Small repairs such as replacing slipped tiles or re-sealing flashing usually cost a few hundred euro. Bigger repairs and replacements depend on the size and condition of the roof. We inspect for free and give you a clear written quote before any work starts.

Is the roof inspection and quote free?

Yes. Inspections and quotes are free and there is no obligation.

What makes you an eco roofing company?

We use sustainable, recyclable materials where we can, recycle old tiles, slates and felt instead of dumping them, use environmentally responsible roof treatments, and repair rather than replace when a repair will do the job.

Does roof cleaning damage the tiles?

Not when it is done properly. We choose the method to suit your roof, soft washing for delicate slate and careful cleaning for concrete tiles, and never blast at high pressure. A biocide treatment then keeps moss away for up to five years.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Dublin, plus Wicklow, Kildare, Meath and Louth, including Bray, Greystones, Naas, Celbridge, Maynooth, Navan, Ashbourne and Drogheda.

Are you insured and is the work guaranteed?

Yes. We carry full public liability insurance and all our work comes with a guarantee.
